# Regeneration clamp capture

**Unrouted, unqualified circuit study. Do not fabricate or power a motor from this
checkpoint.** The original 38-component circuit compiles in atopile 0.15.9; its
105 physical pin connections match the independent primary-pin contract. Five
deliberate wiring faults are rejected by the graph checks. This is connectivity
and bounded arithmetic evidence, not proof that the assembled board is safe.

The clamp connects directly across protected **PACK**, the same node that feeds
the DRV8874. The BQ25798 battery pin also connects to this node and has a 20 V
absolute maximum. A nominal 14 V threshold leaves margin above normal 12.6 V
pack voltage. TLV6710 OUTB drives a PNP that charges the primary AON7400A gate;
two 100 kΩ resistors discharge it. The 340 kΩ / 10 kΩ divider and 20 MΩ feedback
give positive hysteresis. The second comparator input is grounded and its output
is intentionally unused. [TI TLV6710](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv6710.pdf)

Eight 10 Ω Bourns CRM2512 resistors form four parallel branches of two, making
5 Ω. A second AON7400A in series with the dump returns to PACK−. Passive
82 kΩ / 100 kΩ gate bias keeps this path available while the independent thermal
circuit starts. That bias provides at least 4.86 V gate drive at a 9 V pack under
the stated tolerance/temperature allowance. [AON7400A](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AON7400A.pdf)

TPS70933 supplies the thermostat directly from PACK, independently of main SYS
and MCU power. EN is intentionally open; its internal pull-up enables it. EN
must never be tied to the pack voltage. The 4.7 µF output capacitor has a
manufacturer-curve-based effective estimate of 2.69 µF at 3.366 V after separate
tolerance, temperature and aging allowances; TI requires at least 1.5 µF for
this output voltage. The curve is typical, so final stability remains a gate.
[TI TPS709 §8.1.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps709.pdf),
[Samsung CL21A475KAQNNN](https://product.samsungsem.com/mlcc/CL21A475KAQNNN.do).

TMP302A is wired for 65 °C with 10 °C hysteresis. A hot indication starts a
two-MOSFET latch: one holds the indication low, the other pulls the series power
FET gate off. Cooling does not restart the dump. Recovery requires deliberate
removal of PACK power and diagnosis of the fault. A 100 nF PNP base-emitter
capacitor filters startup glitches; it is not a qualified ready timer.
[TI TMP302](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmp302.pdf)

`thermal_ok` is high at the independent local 3.3 V rail when cold, low after a
thermal trip, and has no powered pull-up when that supply is absent. The parent
controller must receive it with powered-off-tolerant hardware logic and gate
motor permission. Do not connect it to an unpowered MCU pin without reviewing
injection current. The local output capacitor discharges over time; loss of PACK
is not claimed to produce an instantaneous low transition.

The explicit `thermal_trip_drive` signal may drive one further AO3400A gate in
the parent controller. That transistor can pull the shared motor-fault line low
without joining two independently powered pull-ups. The latch experiment
includes a third 1 nF gate-load allowance. Do not pull this gate signal up to a
different supply.

## Quantified limits

| Check | Result and scope |
|---|---|
| Rising threshold | 13.36–14.68 V, resistor tolerance plus 100 °C TCR allowance and selected comparator limits |
| Falling threshold | 12.81–14.34 V, including an assumed 0.05–0.5 V PNP on-state drop |
| Conditional transient | 16.22 V at assumed 2 A return, 176 µF, 0.2 Ω ESR and 100 µs total response |
| Response requirement | Less than 257 µs under those assumptions to remain below the 18 V design ceiling |
| Long response failure | A full 1 ms delay gives a 26.44 V arithmetic estimate; it must not be treated as acceptable |
| Stuck-on dump | About 32.1 W at 12.6 V, approximately 4.01 W per resistor |
| Cutoff-open limitation | At 2 A into 176 µF, 18→20 V takes only 176 µs if current continues |
| Standby calculation | Partial bound about 170 µA at 12.6 V, excluding several leakage/loaded-LDO terms |

These results are generated by `hw/sim/blind-regen/checks.py`. TLV6710 timing is
specified typically, without a maximum delay guarantee; the 100 µs response is a
design assumption that must be measured. Input-bias use of ±25 nA is conservative
relative to the published test points, but is not a separately guaranteed
400 mV row. The PNP's low-current saturation behavior also needs confirmation.
The feedback's high impedance makes cleanliness and leakage relevant.

The Bourns 2 W continuous rating requires **300 mm² pad/trace area per resistor**.
The bank's eight parts therefore do not establish a 16 W continuous rating on
this compact PCB. The single-pulse graph supports conservative visual readings
of 20 W at 100 ms and 5 W at 1 s per part, subject to its stated conditions.
The initial design envelope permits only a **provisional 100 ms individual
pulse**, with repetition and cooling still unqualified. This is unsuitable for
unmeasured sustained lowering regeneration. [Bourns CRM, pp. 1–3](https://www.bourns.com/docs/Product-Datasheets/CRM.pdf)

The eight resistor courtyards occupy about 272 mm²; all clamp courtyards total
about 329 mm². Neither total includes additional thermal spreading or routing.
The thermostat must sense the actual hottest bank region, with a measured bound
on gradients and delay. A 65 °C sensor trip does not prove a 65 °C maximum
resistor, cell, enclosure or touch temperature. The single-pulse curve cannot
prove that a stuck dump trips before damage.

## Unresolved release gates

- Measure motor return current/energy, including lowering and a full pack.
- Qualify response, switch SOA, parasitic overshoot, bulk-capacitor ESR/aging,
  hot/cold startup, leakage and brownout across the operating range.
- Measure resistor-to-sensor thermal delay, printed-plastic and cell heating,
  repetition limits, and stuck-primary-FET behavior using a protected fixture.
- Prove the mechanical system prevents sustained back-drive after thermal
  cutoff or pack disconnection. Cutting `nSLEEP` coasts the motor; it does not
  absorb external mechanical energy. A bench fixture must independently limit
  voltage and current before injecting these faults.
- Assess shorted series FET, failed thermostat supply/sensor, and PCB damage.
  The two-stage design is not a functional-safety claim. Pack temperature
  protection is a slower backup, not proof of this local cutoff.

No fabrication, purchase, physical test or production qualification occurred.

## Reproduce the checkpoint

From this directory, with atopile 0.15.9:

```sh
ato build --build regen-test
ato build --build regen-test --frozen
```

From the repository root:

```sh
python3 hw/sim/blind-regen/checks.py --check
python3 hw/sim/blind-regen/run_spice.py
```

Public JLC sourcing evidence is in `bom/electronics-regen-sourcing.json`.
All seven new SKUs were offered for Economic and Standard assembly when checked;
overseas availability does not mean in-house inventory or a final assembly quote.
