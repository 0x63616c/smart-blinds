# Through-hole 21700 holder

MYOUNG BH-21700-A5BJ001, JLC C20606809. **UNVERIFIED assembly and current capacity.**
The manufacturer drawing WD-CP-0332 Rev A/0, dated 2023-08-14, is available from
[LCSC](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/ea18f085f5f9b6da9d587816ecf7f47a.pdf).
The original supplier import is retained in `blind-holders.pretty`; the adjacent
atomic footprint is regenerated by `make_wrappers.py`.

The drawing specifies an 83±0.8 ×25±0.5 mm body, 15.6±0.5 mm unloaded height,
80 mm terminal pitch, 1×3.7 mm PCB terminal slots, two 4 mm PCB mounting holes
at diagonally opposed (±34.3,∓8.5) coordinates, and a 2 mm locating hole.
The corrected footprint makes all three mechanical holes nonplated and retains
them. Its 1.5×4.2 mm copper lands and 0.5 mm courtyard margin are design choices.
Pin1 at X=-40 is positive; pin2 at X=40 is negative, component-side view.

The holder's body bores are drawn as 3.2 mm with 5 mm rings, while the PCB holes
are 4 mm. Their exact fastening/locator use must be inspected on actual hardware;
the drawing does not prove that inserting cells bypasses the solder joints.
The 4±0.5 mm terminals require controlled trimming in the opposed-carrier stack.

The drawing gives **100 mΩ maximum contact resistance**, without an unambiguous
whole-holder versus per-contact definition or a current rating. It does not
prove reverse-insertion rejection for flat-top Samsung 50E cells. Generic ABS
notes also conflict with nylon material fields: do not assume reflow suitability.
Secondary through-hole assembly, retention, contact heating and cell fit remain
qualification gates. No live insertion or arbitrary replacement-cell claim.

Placement review uses an 84.3 × 26.0 mm courtyard: the drawing's maximum
83.8 × 25.5 mm body plus 0.25 mm per side. The earlier 84.8 × 26.5 mm
reservation was a provisional extra margin, not a manufacturer requirement;
it falsely overlapped holders spaced 26 mm apart. Actual maximum bodies retain
0.5 mm between neighbours. The corner support boss has only 0.2 mm nominal
clearance to the maximum holder envelope, so printed tolerances and assembly
remain qualification gates. Reducing the planning courtyard does not establish
physical fit, insertion-force support or reverse-polarity rejection.
