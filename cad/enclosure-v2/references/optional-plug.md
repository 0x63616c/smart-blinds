# Optional removable adjustment plug

Status: OPEN, cosmetic candidate only. It leaves the selected integral button, its4.2 mm access bore and all adjustment/assembly geometry unchanged. A white printed5.8 mm cap with a3 mm stem covers the recess. A2.5 mm groove carries one2.5 ID×1 mm silicone ring; nominal4.5 OD compressed into4.2 gives15% radial squeeze. This is explicit elastic retention, **not** a rigid interference, a proven pull force or an ingress seal.

A0.4 mm-high notch under one cap edge receives an allocated0.3×1.6 mm nonconductive blade. The nominal straight approach and the hard plug's complete10 mm withdrawal clear the actual integral button. The ring must deform during installation/removal; its assembly over the3 mm shoulder entails20% temporary ID stretch. Neither this stretch nor friction is physically qualified. The hard stem remains2.05 mm above the highest proposed adjustment screw head.

The cap adds0.7 mm projection (79.5 including buttons,77 mm shell), two printed pieces and two rings per blind. Thus24 installed pieces return, but the cosmetic face does not need adhesive and no nut wrench is required. These small plugs need retention/creep, ring tear/rolling, printer accuracy and repeated-service tests. Keep them out of the required BOM until that test; the underlying button remains adjustable and usable without them.
